\# Contributing



Hey, thanks for even being here - that already means a lot! 🖤



\## Got a bug or idea?



Open an issue and tell me about it! No template, no rules, just talk to me.



\## Want to send a Pull Request?



Go for it! Just try to keep it focused on one thing so it's easy to review.  

Branch off `main`, do your thing, send it back.



\## Adding a new mod?



Put it under `src/your-mod-name/` with its own `README.md` and `mod\_info.yaml`.  

Check \[ExpressiveDupes](src/ExpressiveDupes) to see how it's structured.



\## Credit



Add your name wherever feels right. You helped, you deserve it.
